RESEARCH DIRECTION / 01

AI驱动物理发现和材料设计AI-Driven Physical Discovery and Materials Design

把机器学习从材料性质的预测器，推进为能够提出物理描述、探索材料空间并反向设计新候选的研究工具。We use machine learning not only to predict material properties, but also to formulate physical descriptors, explore materials space, and design new candidates in reverse.

这个方向连接可解释的力学描述符、通用机器学习势、高通量有限温度模拟与全空间逆向设计，关注材料为什么呈现出目标性质，也关注如何以更少的计算代价找到真正值得验证的新结构。This direction connects interpretable mechanical descriptors, universal machine-learning potentials, high-throughput finite-temperature simulations, and full-space inverse design. We study why materials exhibit target properties and how to identify structures worth validating with less computational cost.

01 / MATERIALS INVERSE DESIGN

材料逆向设计Materials Inverse Design

从目标性质出发，在元素类型、化学配比和结构构型共同定义的庞大材料空间中寻找合适的候选。Starting from target properties, we search for suitable candidates across the vast space defined by elements, stoichiometry, and structure.

全空间（A、C、S）· 性质导向搜索FULL (A, C, S) SPACE · PROPERTY-DRIVEN SEARCH

从目标性质出发，探索材料的全空间逆向设计Exploring full-space materials inverse design from target properties

材料的性质由元素类型（A）、化学配比（C）和结构构型（S）共同决定，三者的组合构成庞大的设计空间，给具有目标性质的新材料发现带来挑战。我们团队聚焦材料逆向设计，从目标性质出发寻找合适的化学组成与原子排列，不局限于已知化合物或预设结构原型。Material properties are jointly determined by elemental identity (A), chemical stoichiometry (C), and atomic structure (S). Their combination forms a vast design space that makes the discovery of materials with target properties challenging. We focus on inverse design, starting from target properties to identify suitable compositions and atomic arrangements without being limited to known compounds or predefined structural prototypes.

我们发展了联合探索（A、C、S）全空间的逆向设计方法：利用全局优化算法引导目标性质导向的搜索，通过机器学习原子间势与性质预测模型快速评估候选材料，并通过主动学习协同推进材料探索与模型迭代优化。We develop inverse-design methods that jointly explore the full (A, C, S) space. Global optimization guides property-driven searches, machine-learning interatomic potentials and property-prediction models rapidly evaluate candidates, and active learning couples materials exploration with iterative model refinement.

相关方法已应用于晶体结构预测、新型超硬 B–C–N 化合物发现、全空间功能材料设计，以及通用机器学习势的数据采样与模型训练。通过结合先进的机器学习与优化算法，我们致力于寻找兼顾多性质要求的新材料，发展可靠、可迁移的逆向设计方法。These methods have been applied to crystal structure prediction, the discovery of novel superhard B–C–N compounds, full-space functional-materials design, and data sampling and model training for universal machine-learning potentials. By combining advanced machine learning with optimization algorithms, we seek materials that satisfy multiple property requirements and reliable, transferable inverse-design methods.

设计要素 全局优化 · MLIP · 性质预测 · 主动学习 · A–C–S 全空间

Design elements Global optimization · MLIP · property prediction · active learning · full (A, C, S) space

02 / PHYSICAL DISCOVERY

物理发现Physical Discovery

围绕材料宏观性质背后的物理机制，从超硬材料和负热膨胀材料出发，寻找可解释、可迁移的结构-性质规律。We investigate the physical mechanisms behind macroscopic material properties, using superhard and negative-thermal-expansion materials to uncover interpretable and transferable structure-property relationships.

超硬材料 · C–N 可变组成SUPERHARD MATERIALS · C–N VARIABLE COMPOSITION

用全局机器学习势和多目标优化，反向设计超硬 C–N 化合物Inverse design of superhard C–N compounds with global machine-learning potentials and multiobjective optimization

钻石的硬度为新型超硬材料设定了很高的参照，但传统晶体结构预测往往需要预先指定组成或结构。这里建立可变组成逆向设计流程，让机器学习势在搜索过程中持续更新，并同时处理离散的化学计量与连续的晶体结构。Diamond provides a demanding benchmark for new superhard materials, while conventional crystal-structure prediction often requires a predefined composition or structure. We establish a variable-composition inverse-design workflow in which machine-learning potentials are updated during the search and discrete stoichiometry is optimized together with continuous crystal structures.

经过多轮搜索，研究发现了 38 种此前不在主要材料数据库中的稳定 C–N 超硬材料，其中 C3（P6322）预测硬度达到 97.4 GPa，超过文中采用的金刚石参照值。After multiple search cycles, we identified 38 stable C–N superhard materials that were not included in the main materials databases. The predicted hardness of C3 (P6322) reaches 97.4 GPa, exceeding the diamond reference used in the study.

核心结果 变量组成逆向设计 · 38 个新候选 · 97.4 GPa

Key results Variable-composition inverse design · 38 new candidates · 97.4 GPa

负热膨胀 · 通用描述符NEGATIVE THERMAL EXPANSION · UNIVERSAL DESCRIPTOR

用剪切-成键描述符绘制负热膨胀材料的可解释图景An interpretable picture of negative thermal expansion from a shear-bond descriptor

负热膨胀材料对于降低器件中的热应力非常重要，但这类材料长期缺少能够跨化学空间迁移的设计原则。研究将百余年的热膨胀数据与机器学习结合，找到由剪切模量和成键模量构成的可解释描述符。Negative-thermal-expansion materials are important for reducing thermal stress in devices, yet design principles that transfer across chemical space have remained limited. We combine more than a century of thermal-expansion data with machine learning to identify an interpretable descriptor built from shear and bond moduli.

这个描述符把正、负热膨胀材料放入同一张力学图景，并指导通用机器学习势驱动的高通量有限温度模拟。由此预测了 3,665 种具有宽工作温区的负热膨胀晶体，随后合成和表征了 11 种此前未报道的化合物，验证了框架的可迁移性。The descriptor places positive- and negative-expansion materials in a common mechanical picture and guides high-throughput finite-temperature simulations powered by universal machine-learning potentials. We predicted 3,665 negative-thermal-expansion crystals with broad operating ranges and experimentally synthesized and characterized 11 previously unreported compounds.

核心结果 3,665 个候选 · 11 个实验验证 · 可解释 G/Ē 描述符

Key results 3,665 candidates · 11 experimental validations · interpretable G/Ē descriptor

03 / WATER AND NANOCONFINED TRANSPORT

水与纳米限域输运Water and Nanoconfined Transport

从分子尺度理解限域如何重塑相互作用、动力学与输运，并寻找调控纳流体摩擦、水扩散和水活化的微观路径。We study how confinement reshapes molecular interactions, dynamics, and transport at the molecular scale, seeking mechanisms to control nanofluidic friction, water diffusion, and water activation.

微观机制 · 输运调控MICROSCOPIC MECHANISMS · TRANSPORT CONTROL

纳米限域材料中输运的微观机制及调控优化

Microscopic mechanisms and ways to control/optimize transport in nanoconfined materials

中文概述

我们研究微观和纳米尺度限域如何重塑分子相互作用、动力学与输运，重点关注水及水溶液界面。研究揭示了分子柔性、氢键网络重构和电子结构在极端限域下调控纳流体摩擦、水扩散和水活化中的作用。

通过结合第一性原理模拟与机器学习分子动力学，我们致力于揭示限域体系中输运与反应性的调控机制。

English overview

We investigate how micro- and nanoscale confinement reshapes molecular interactions, dynamics, and transport, with a focus on water and aqueous interfaces. Our work reveals the roles of molecular flexibility, hydrogen-bond network reconstruction, and electronic structure in governing nanofluidic friction, water diffusion, and water activation under extreme confinement.

By combining first-principles simulations and machine-learning molecular dynamics, we seek to uncover molecular mechanisms for controlling transport and reactivity in confined systems.
